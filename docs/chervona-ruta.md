# Червона рута · Orchestra

Choose **Червона рута · Orchestra** in Groove's song library, load it and enable
Song mode to play the complete arrangement. Music and lyrics: **Volodymyr
Ivasiuk (Володимир Івасюк)**. This instrumental adaptation follows the four
voice-and-piano score scans supplied for this contribution; it does not contain
a vocal recording or the scanned images.

The 16 editable lanes use flute, trumpet, two horns, tuba, trombone, two violin
parts, viola, two cello parts, two harp parts, snare, cymbals and low toms. Install
the VSCO orchestra and Virtuosity Drums recordings using the
[instrument installation instructions](../web/instruments/README.md#local-installation-and-native-packaging).
The public source includes the song and instrument manifests. The recordings
are installed separately. Missing recordings produce the usual instrument error.

The adaptation stays in G minor, 4/4, at a chosen moderate tempo of 104 BPM,
without swing. The vocal line becomes the first violin melody, joined by flute
and trumpet; the chorus's second voice goes to second violins. Harp preserves
the opening motif and selected piano answers. The remaining parts expand the
piano harmony into strings and brass, with light percussion in the choruses.

The 86 bars expand the score's repeat and alternate endings:

| Passage | Written bars | Played bars |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prelude and pickup | unnumbered opening through 8 | 9 |
| Verse 1 | 9–25 | 17 |
| Chorus and first ending | 26–40 | 15 |
| Verse 2 | 9–25 | 17 |
| Final chorus | 26–39 | 14 |
| Second ending and echoes | 41–48 | 8 |
| Diminuendo and final tonic | 49–54 | 6 |

This is an orchestral adaptation, with newly voiced accompaniment. The engine
uses a fixed tempo: the printed *poco rit.* and final fermata are represented by
a quieter, sparse coda and a final held bar. The last tonic is softly
rearticulated because the sequencer limits individual gates to one bar.
Playback lasts about 3:18 plus the release tail. Project saving and song WAV
export preserve the complete arrangement; MIDI export uses the selected pattern.
