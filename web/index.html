MPK mini Plus II · Auto setup

Connect USB, select DAW mode on the MPK and press “Enable ready DAW profile”. Assignments, display and lights configure themselves and are saved on this Mac. A modified User Preset has its own guided learning.

Select DAW mode on the MPK, then the ready profile. Display: song, your instrument, tempo and knob bank. Pads: track colour, mute and activity. Play, Record, Loop and Tap show their state. PAD BANK A/B switches the physical bank; it is not a pad.

Pads → tracks · Beat mode

In Beat mode a pad plays its assigned track. During playback each hit is written to the nearest 1/16 step of the pattern you hear. UNDO on the MPK removes the last pass. Hold the central encoder and press a pad to select a track.

    Held central encoder + pad selects a track. BANK −/+ switch knob banks; SHIFT + knob is fine adjustment. Track sound / mixer / motion / rhythm control the selected track; Live Synth / Synth extra control the instrument you play. To play with the same sound, enable “Follow selected part” in Synth. Track tone changes are heard from the next note; mixer and effects change while sounding. Track settings are saved with the song.

    Buttons & SHIFT · cheat sheet

    Held central encoder + pad: select a track (A1–A8 → 1–8, B1–B8 → 9–16); its name shows on the display and the selected pad lights yellow. SHIFT + pad belongs to the MPK itself (pad modes, Prog, Chord Type): if such a menu opens, press SHIFT + the same pad again. If the MPK switched to Plugin or User Presets, press PLUGIN/DAW without SHIFT. PAD BANK A/B switches the pad bank. Encoder: your instrument; SHIFT + encoder: previous / next pattern. Push (release without a pad): start / confirm a jam; SHIFT + push: cancel. SHIFT + BANK −/+: choose a song, Play: load and start it. SHIFT + Record: record keys into the selected track. BANK −/+ change the bank of 8 knobs; SHIFT + knobs: fine adjustment. Play: play / stop; SHIFT + Play: continue. Record: record / finish. Undo / SHIFT + Undo: undo / redo. Loop: repeat the song; SHIFT + Loop: stop all sound. Tap: tempo; SHIFT + Tap: metronome. +: quantize. SHIFT + PAD BANK: pad mode. OCT, ARP, LATCH, CHORDS, SCALES and NOTE REPEAT are handled by the MPK itself; play them through the MIDI port. To sync ARP, choose a MIDI clock output under USB MIDI.

      1 · Choose a song and press Load track, or open your own JSON. 2 · Press ▶ Play. 3 · Play the MPK over the song.

      Engine strip
      Click Groove, Recording or Ardour to choose what the transport controls. Switching workspace never changes it.
      Auto sync
      In Groove, Auto sync track detects the loaded recording’s beats locally. Press Play to hear both; Groove follows the recording’s seeks, loops and speed. Manual BPM changes unlink them. Estimated beats can be half/double time and do not identify the musical downbeat.
      Separate clock
      Engines run independently unless Groove is synced to the recording. Ardour always has its own clock.
