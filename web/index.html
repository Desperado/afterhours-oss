Eight expressive macros · selected Groove lanes

Weight adds low end; Bite adds saturation and harmonics; Drift varies pitch and tone; Width spreads lanes; Air opens filters and shelves; Tension opens filters and tightens decay; Space feeds shared echo and reverb; Pulse adds rhythmic movement. Original patches stay editable.

USB MIDI + MIDI Learn
Connect a keyboard. Right-click a Controller, Synth, macro or mixer knob to Learn.

    Velocity, sustain, ±2-semitone pitch wheel, channel pressure and poly aftertouch play Synth. Hardware knobs use pickup: cross the software value to take control. Mappings travel with the project. SysEx is disabled.

    Lane mixer + shared effects

    Three-band EQ, pan, gain, mute, solo and sends per lane. Delay and reverb share a music return. Mixer and macros are included in Groove WAV and selected-lane stem exports. Stem export is in Groove → Export → Track WAV.

    Modulation playground

    Four routes add motion above the saved controller values. LFOs, envelopes and random movement follow tempo; lane amplitude follows its meter. Depth zero disables the effect. Modulation does not write knob automation into your base setup.

    Engine strip
    Click Groove, Recording or Ardour to choose what the transport controls. Switching workspace never changes it.
    Auto sync
    In Groove, Auto sync track detects the loaded recording’s beats locally. Press Play to hear both; Groove follows the recording’s seeks, loops and speed. Manual BPM changes unlink them. Estimated beats can be half/double time and do not identify the musical downbeat.
    Separate clock
    Engines run independently unless Groove is synced to the recording. Ardour always has its own clock.
