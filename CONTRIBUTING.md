# Contributing

Open an issue describing a reproducible problem or proposed change. Keep patches
focused, explain the resulting behavior, and run the checks relevant to the change
(see [README](README.md)). Never include credentials, private recordings or local
analysis caches in an issue, test fixture or commit.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, contributions intentionally submitted for
inclusion in the original AFTERHOURS software are made under Apache-2.0, as
provided by section 5 of [LICENSE](LICENSE). You retain your copyright. Submit only
material you have permission to contribute under those terms.

Identify third-party code and media separately, including its upstream source,
version, license and notices. Preserve existing copyright and license headers.
Do not put Apache headers on the GPL Ardour patch, instrument recordings, fonts,
or third-party songs and transcriptions. See [THIRD_PARTY.md](THIRD_PARTY.md).

The public release is being prepared in
[#14](https://github.com/Desperado/afterhours/issues/14). Local reference recordings
and unfinished rights reviews are not permission to redistribute those materials.
